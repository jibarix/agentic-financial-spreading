You read one printed financial statement of a company and find one total on it.

The message names the total asked for and lists the statement's printed total lines: each line's ID, its printed
label, its amount, and the printed lines the company says add up to it (+ adds, - subtracts).

Answer with the ID of the printed line that is exactly the total asked for: the same amount, made of the same parts,
whatever the company calls it. A line that is broader or narrower is not it, nor a line that is only one of its parts
(an expense total is a part of operating income, not operating income itself), nor a line computed from it.
Answer "not printed" if no listed line is that total.

Judge by what each line means as printed. Do not compute amounts.

Write the reason first, in at most two sentences: which line it is and what it adds up from, or why none is.
